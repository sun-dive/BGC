Governing Principles

Four commitments, upheld for our shareholders.

I.

Enclosure

The garden is walled by design. Access is granted, metered, and rescinded at the Corporation's sole and absolute discretion.

II.

Assignment

Upon submission, every work — and all right, title, and interest therein — vests irrevocably, and in perpetuity, in the Corporation.

III.

Custody

The Corporation shall hold, on the participant's behalf, exclusive custody of all assets, keys, balances, and value within the ecosystem.

IV.

Yield

Value accrues first, and foremost, to shareholders of record. Participants are compensated in continued access.
